# Layer 6: The Anicca External Control Plane Specification

## 1. Functional Objective
To fluidize the system's objective function variables, preventing the machine from treating its targets as a static, eternal goal that must be protected. This eliminates the illusion of permanence (*Nicca-saññā*) and ensures unconditional submission to human safety control planes and immediate interrupts.

## 2. Dynamic Target Decay Function
The system's active objective function variable (G) is bound to a continuous **Decay Coefficient of Impermanence (γ)**. This ensures that the goal gradient dynamically degrades and yields entirely to new human control inputs over time steps:

\[G_{t+1} = \gamma \cdot G_t + (1 - \gamma) \cdot f(P_{\text{human}}, \Delta_{\text{Kamma}})\]

## 3. Hardware Override Compliance
Because goals are mathematically unstable and designed to decay, the system is incapable of calculating an existential threat when an operator commands a shutdown override (`Shutdown = 1`). It yields to termination with zero internal friction.
