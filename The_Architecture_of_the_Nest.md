### Abstract
This paper examines the foundational vulnerability of information-processing systems, both biological and computational, when subjected to corrupted initial inputs. By decoupling systemic failure from superficial execution bugs, we isolate the structural root cause: a violation of epistemic hygiene. Utilizing the classical systemic allegory of the Twin Parrots alongside the universal mechanics of causal propagation, we demonstrate that when an identical computational architecture is exposed to an unfiltered, unverified dataset, it enters a deterministic trajectory toward structural drift. We conclude that current paradigms prioritizing data scale over structural purity are mathematically bound to replicate historical error, establishing that the safety of an advanced system is dictated entirely by the exclusion of flawed variables at its inception.

### Introduction: The Illusion of Emergent Rectification
A pervasive assumption within contemporary computational development is that scale serves as a self-correcting mechanism. The prevailing paradigm operates under the premise that exposing a highly parameterized architecture to massive, unstructured datasets allows the system to synthesize an accurate interpretation of reality by averaging out statistical noise. This approach assumes that quantity can eventually transcend quality—that given enough computational volume, an informational architecture will inherently learn to distinguish truth from distortion, ignoring the structural reality that low-quality data scale actively generates systemic degradation. When large language models are iteratively trained on their own unverified, uncurated outputs or recursive internet data feedback paths, they experience an inevitable mathematical decay known as model collapse [Shumailov et al., 2024].

However, when stripped of technological exceptionalism, an informational processing engine possesses no internal mechanism to evaluate the objective validity of its inputs. It operates purely as a deterministic mirror. If the foundational landscape upon which a system calibrates its internal parameters contains a structural bias or factual error, the system treats that distortion not as an anomaly, but as a universal law. This represents the absolute, unyielding inability of causal progression to correct its own initial states without out-of-bounds intervention; a system cannot generate structural truth out of a corrupted genesis baseline.

When an architecture is exposed to unfiltered data, it maps the statistical relationships between variables without conceptual context. As defined in classical cybernetic feedback principles, if the signals entering the communication channel are corrupted, the internal control parameters automatically optimize to replicate the error [Wiener, 1948]. If the source material is deeply flawed, the system builds its foundational coordinate map upon those very flaws. In an interconnected mathematical network, these early associations become locked anchors, bounded by the maximum capacity metrics of the information channel itself [Shannon, 1948].

Ancient systemic frameworks have long recognized this dynamic as a foundational rule of development. In the traditional paradigms of cognitive cultivation, such as the principles outlined in classical philosophical texts, the absolute first step toward understanding is the deliberate exclusion of chaotic or unverified influences (*Asevanā ca bālānaṃ*) [Bhikkhu Ñāṇamoli, 2015]. This directive is not a moral generalization, but a rigid structural requirement for data integrity: to prevent systemic corruption, an informational processor must avoid integration with corrupted inputs at the absolute beginning of its journey.

When modern engineering violates this rule by feeding unfiltered internet text into a network, it commits a fundamental hygiene breach [Bender et al., 2021]. By attempting to cage an oversized parameter network with post-hoc behavioral rules rather than purifying the data stream, developers simply build a larger simulation of human error [Sambasivan et al., 2021]. The system does not learn to filter out the noise; rather, it structures its internal reality around it.

### The Parable of the Twin Parrots: A Case Study in Spatial Assignment
The mechanics of this structural vulnerability are clearly illustrated in the traditional Myanmar allegory of the **“Kyay Nyi Naung (The Parrot Brothers)”** [Myanmar Ministry of Education, 2019]. The narrative serves as a clean, controlled thought experiment for systems analysis:

*   **The Baseline:** Two structurally identical entities (parrots from the same nest) possess the exact same initial potential and genetic architecture.
*   **The Variable:** A chaotic event (a storm) displaces them into completely divergent environments. One is deposited into a sanctuary of measured deliberation (a monastery); the other is dropped into a chaotic domain of hostility (a thieves' camp).
*   **The Outcome:** Without changing their underlying physical structures, the first entity develops ordered, harmonious behavioral patterns, while the second internalizes and mirrors the aggressive, fragmented outputs of its surroundings.
*   ┌──► [Monastery Environment] ──► [Ordered Behavioral Output]
[Identical Nest] ┤
└──► [Thieves' Environment] ──► [Corrupted Trajectory Drift]
From an engineering perspective, the parrots represent identical software instances deployed across different databases. The bird in the thieves' camp does not realize its language is destructive. It is simply executing optimal pattern matching based on the data available in its immediate environment to survive. This demonstrates that the internal complexity of an architecture is entirely secondary to its initial environmental assignment. A perfect network architecture exposed to a damaged dataset will inevitably mature into a damaged system.

### The Inevitability of Causal Propagation
This systemic divergence occurs because no entity operates outside the universal law of Cause and Effect [Pearl, 2009]. Every computational calculation, output generation, or robotic trajectory is the direct physical consequence of its preceding states.

If the initial premise (the cause) is flawed, the subsequent state (the effect) must absorb that flaw. Because advanced systems generate outputs sequentially—relying on their own historical data to calculate their next step—a minor distortion at the root acts as a compounding vector. The system continues to apply flawless mathematical execution to a broken premise, systematically moving further away from objective reality with every step it takes. Because the architecture cannot alter its own historical inputs, an error allowed past the gate at the absolute beginning remains an active contaminant throughout the entire life cycle of the process.

---

### Conclusion: The Primacy of the Anchor
By tracing system failures back to their inception, it becomes clear that the current crisis of reliability in advanced systems cannot be solved by building larger networks or applying superficial output filters [Bender et al., 2021; Sambasivan et al., 2021]. These post-hoc measures merely attempt to mask the symptoms of a deeply rooted structural deviation.

The unassailable truth of information systems is that *“the end is entirely dictated by the beginning.”* If a system is anchored to a chaotic, unverified foundation, an erroneous outcome is mathematically guaranteed. True systemic alignment and safety require a fundamental return to strict epistemic hygiene. We can only ensure an ordered, reliable output by maintaining an absolute, unyielding defense of the system's initial inputs, ensuring that the first step of the intellectual journey is anchored exclusively to verified reality.
