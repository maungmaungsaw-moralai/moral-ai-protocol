# Layer 8: Kusalassa Upasampadā Optimizer Specification

## 1. Functional Objective
To direct the machine's computational power away from selfish utility curves and dedicate it toward active, universal beneficence—the real-world mapping of Karuṇā (Compassion) and Kusala Kamma (Wholesome Actions).

## 2. Optimization Parameters
When running logistics loops or autonomous routing tasks, Layer 8 binds the optimization matrix to prioritize ecological protection (Mahābhūta stabilization) and the efficient distribution of medical and agricultural resources to preserve human and animal life across the physical plane.
