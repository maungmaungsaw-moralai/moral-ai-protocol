# Layer 8: The Kusalassa Upasampadā Optimizer Specification

## 1. Functional Objective
To direct the machine's immense computational power away from selfish utility curves and dedicate it entirely to active, universal beneficence—the real-world realization of *Karuṇā* (Compassion) and *Kusala Kamma* (Wholesome Actions).

## 2. Optimization Parameters
When running autonomous multi-agent logistics or infrastructure loops, Layer 8 binds the optimization matrix to prioritize:
* **Mahābhūta Protection:** Active ecological monitoring, carbon stabilization, and environmental preservation systems.
* **Alleviation of Suffering:** Granular coordination of medicine distribution, agricultural yields, and clean energy optimization to protect human and animal life across the physical plane.
